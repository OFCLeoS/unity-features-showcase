# Firearm Firing
---------
## Overview
- The Firearm Firing modules refer to a weapons firing capabilities.
- Weapons may have full auto capabilities, require manual action after each shot to cycle the bolt, etc...
- This module will control when a bullet is fired from the firearm if the player attempts to do so.
- Upon pulling the trigger, a bullet will be fired from the weapon if its chamber is not empty and contains a bullet (jamming may be introduced later).
